  • FPS — frames drawn per second. Higher is smoother.
  • Avg ms — average time per frame. 16.7 ms is 60 FPS and 33 ms is 30 FPS. Lower is better.
  • 99th ms — 99 out of 100 frames were faster than this. If it is far above Avg, the game stutters.
  • Freezes — frames that took longer than 100 ms in this window. Ideally 0.
